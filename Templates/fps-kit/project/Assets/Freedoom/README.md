# Freedoom licensing

Most of the art and sound in this kit comes from [Freedoom](https://freedoom.github.io/), which is under a
BSD-3-Clause licence. You can use it in your own game, change it and sell the result, as long as you follow the
conditions below.

This is a plain-language summary and not legal advice. The licence itself is in `COPYING.txt`, and that text is what
counts.

## What you need to do

1. **Keep the licence with your game.** Anyone you give your game to must also get Freedoom's copyright notice,
   conditions and disclaimer, which are all in `COPYING.txt`.
2. **Don't use Freedoom's name to promote your game.** You can't use the name of the Freedoom project or its
   contributors to endorse or promote your game without written permission. Saying in your credits that your game
   uses Freedoom's art is fine.

Freedoom comes with no warranty, as the disclaimer in `COPYING.txt` explains.

## Exporting your game

Tilky copies the whole `Assets` folder when you export, so this folder goes with your game automatically. **Don't
delete or empty it** while your game still uses Freedoom's art or sound, including anything you edited, because the
conditions still apply to edited versions.

It's also a good idea to mention Freedoom in your credits or on your store page, so players can find the licence.
For example:

> Contains art and sound from Freedoom, Copyright © 2001-2024 Contributors to the Freedoom project. See
> `Assets/Freedoom/COPYING.txt`.

If you replace all of Freedoom's art and sound, you no longer need this folder.

## What this licence doesn't cover

These parts of the kit aren't Freedoom's:

- **Music:** the tracks in `Assets/Music` are CC0, from OpenGameArt. See `Assets/Music/CREDITS.txt`.
- **Lettering and numbers:** `Assets/UI/Words`, `Assets/UI/Digits/Tally *` and `Assets/Fonts/Classic HUD.ttf` were
  drawn for this kit, and you can use them however you like.
